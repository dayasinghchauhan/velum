# Changelog

## 0.1.3 (27 Oct 2026)

- Video no longer stops when the camera refuses a setting combination: Velum steps down (Enhanced → Standard
  stabilisation, then HLG → SDR), tells you, and keeps going.
- The camera reopens by itself after a camera error instead of showing a dead viewfinder.
- Heat guard: a warning when the phone gets hot; if it gets critical while recording, the recording stops and is
  saved instead of being cut off by the system.
- Unfinished files left in DCIM/Velum (e.g. from closing the app while slow motion was saving) are cleaned up.
- The colour strip now highlights the right group after choosing Off or a look from All looks.

## 0.1.2 (27 Oct 2026), first public beta

- First public build for testers.
- Signature looks use Velum's own names (Rangefinder, Optic, Studio, Cine). Each group shows what it is inspired by,
  on the camera screen and in the looks sheet.
- Signed with the permanent release key. Future updates install over this version and keep your settings.
